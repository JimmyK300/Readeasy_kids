# Vietnamese lesson adaptation notes

Audited user-owned `public/quiz1.json` picture/word bank and `public/quiz2.json` listening word bank. These were vocabulary exercises, not ready-to-publish reading stories. This extension adapts topics and words into original short Vietnamese comprehension stories, without third-party passages or curriculum-equivalence claims.

| Lesson | Level | Vocabulary/topic source | Reading focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| An và khu vườn | Bắt đầu | First prototype story; cây, nước in quiz1 | Person, reason, action |
| Mèo bên cửa sổ | Bắt đầu | mèo, mặt trời, nhà in quiz1 | Object, place, action |
| Quyển sách của Lan | Bắt đầu | sách, bàn, ghế, cá in quiz1 | Action, detail, sequence |
| Bữa sáng của Bin | Bắt đầu | táo in quiz1; sữa, bánh in quiz2 | Before/after, object, place |
| Quả bóng trong sân | Đọc thêm | bóng, nhà, cây in quiz1 | Place, reason, sequence |
| Chiếc nón của bà | Đọc thêm | nón, bông in quiz2 | Place, evidence, reason |
| Sau cơn mưa | Đọc thêm | mây, trời xanh, hoa, bướm in quiz1 | Observation, place, reason |

Five-sentence beginning and six-sentence longer stories each have three questions. Suggested levels describe length and reasoning, not grade placement or medical classification. All 21 keys manually checked against supporting lines. Automated checks require one matching answer, three unique options, valid supporting line and evidence text present in that line.

Original chair/desk picture keys were swapped; those pictures are excluded. Listening recordings were not sufficiently verified for this reading scope, so no audio keys/media are presented as validated exercises. Legacy English React quizzes, random facts and incomplete activities are excluded from navigation/build entrypoints. Historical material remains source for audit; only mascot/favicon whitelist ships.

Everyday home, friendship and nature contexts allow rereading without a timer. Vietnamese educator and intended-learner review remains necessary before production.
