# Reading path and adult support

ReadEasy remains a founder prototype for Vietnamese reading support, including a dyslexia-oriented ambition without clinical validation. No new content, backend schema or provider change was needed.

The extension retains the lavender/purple identity, yellow accents, Nunito headings, comfortable Segoe reading text and fox/cat assets. Its memorable element is an open three-stage reading path with numbered circles and visible completion counts. It separates a child-friendly next-story suggestion from the adult progress report. All elements wrap down to 280 px content width; no width clipping is used to conceal overflow.

Primary product guidance inspected on 2026-10-08:
- [Khan Academy: understanding progress](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360032987591-How-can-I-understand-my-progress-in-Khan-Academy): dedicated progress navigation and filtered activity views informed the separate adult route.
- [Khan Academy: Individual Student Report](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/7263187791373-How-do-I-use-the-Individual-Student-Report): distinct completion/activity and mastery concepts informed transparent score labels and completed/revisit filtering.

These are navigation ideas only. No assets, curriculum passages, mastery model, branding or affiliation were copied. ReadEasy's three-question scores are not mastery or diagnostic outcomes. The view does not claim to provide Khan Academy's full reporting, class or assignment capabilities.

Suggestions remain optional: resume an unfinished session; reread a completed story with fewer than two correct answers; otherwise try the next unread story. The adult can select any story. A score below three surfaces in the report's reread filter without blocking progression. This is a transparent product heuristic, not an evidence-based educational threshold.
